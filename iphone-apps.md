## Settings

- [ ] **Contacts sync to Google (important: don't skip).** Every personal contact added on the iPhone must save to personal Google Contacts, not iCloud.
  1. Settings → Apps → Contacts → Contacts Accounts → Add Account → Google. Sign in with the personal Gmail and turn on **Contacts**.
  2. Settings → Apps → Contacts → **Default Account** → the personal Gmail account. Never the InHouse Digital Skills Workspace account.
  3. Check: add a test contact on the iPhone and look for it at contacts.google.com a minute later, then delete it.
  4. If iCloud Contacts is on with contacts still in it, export them from icloud.com as a vCard file, import that at contacts.google.com, then turn off iCloud Contacts on the iPhone (choose "Delete from My iPhone"; the iCloud copy stays online).

  Business contacts live in the CRM and reach the phone through the Aircall app, so they stay out of this list.
